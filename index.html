How To Play
🎯 3-STAGE THROW
1 AIM: Drag on the board (or ◀ ▶) to move the target. Lock it.
2 POWER: Bar sweeps up & down — tap LOCK in the sweet spot. Too soft = short. Too hard = long & it flies off.
3 SPIN: Bar sweeps left ↔ right — tap THROW! Spin bends the bag in flight and makes it bite & slide on landing.

🏆 SCORING (Cancellation)
• Bag in the hole = 3 pts  • Bag on board = 1 pt
• Each inning both sides throw 4 bags. Only the difference counts.
• First to 21 wins. Lucky: slide into the hole with spin!
